# **Project Overview: Video Game Sales Analysis**

This project conducts an in-depth Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on a historical dataset of over 16,000 video game sales. The objective is to uncover global industry trends, regional market distributions, genre popularity, and publisher dominance to extract actionable business intelligence for the gaming sector.

## **Technical Implementation**
**Data Wrangling:** Utilized pandas for data ingestion, inspection, and cleaning, which included dropping null values to maintain dataset integrity across 11 continuous and categorical features.

**Exploratory Data Analysis:** Aggregated and grouped sales metrics across multiple dimensions (Year, Genre, Publisher, Region) to evaluate market performance and consumer behavior.

**Data Visualization:** Engineered a suite of visualizations using matplotlib and seaborn. This included time-series bar charts for yearly revenue trends, pie charts for regional market share, and heatmaps for analyzing cross-regional genre performance.

## **Key Business Insights**
**Regional Market Share:** North America heavily dominates the global market, accounting for 49.1% of total sales, followed by Europe (27.3%) and Japan (14.6%).

**Industry Revenue Cycles:** Global sales reached a historical peak in 2008—driven by major console and title releases—before experiencing a gradual decline, highlighting shifts in entertainment consumption.

**Genre & Publisher Dynamics:** The Action genre consistently leads global demand. Furthermore, the publishing landscape is highly consolidated, with Nintendo standing out as the definitive market leader among the top 20 publishers.
